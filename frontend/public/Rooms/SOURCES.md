The following images were downloaded from Wikimedia Commons and Unsplash to provide realistic room images for the `RoomCard` and `RoomDetails` components.

| Room Type | Count | Source | License | Attribution Req |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |

| **Double** | 10 | Unsplash | Unsplash License | No |

*Note: The images are used deterministically via the backend `seedDemoRooms.ts` script to ensure that the first 20 seeded demo rooms have 100% unique primary images, fulfilling the demo requirements.*
